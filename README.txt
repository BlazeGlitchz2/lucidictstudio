LUCID. ICT — THE NETWORK STUDIO
Cambridge IGCSE ICT (0417) | Hodder Third Edition | Printed pages 76–87

OPEN THE WEBSITE
1. Double-click "LUCID_ICT_Network_Studio.html" to open in Safari, Chrome, Edge, etc.
2. Start with "What is a network?" and follow the 11 lessons in order.
3. Use the interactive diagrams, 53 practice questions, flashcards, exam, and revision sheet.
4. You can use the website without internet access.

SCOPE
The lessons are limited to textbook pages 76–87. Detailed passwords,
authentication, anti-malware and conferencing (starting later in the book)
are intentionally excluded. MAN is outside the syllabus according to the text.

IMPORTANT
A teacher worksheet identifying any additional exclusions was not attached.
The prior IGCSE networks study guide available in the library did not identify
what your teacher explicitly excluded. This website therefore uses the textbook
page range only and does not claim worksheet-specific filtering.

LOCAL PROGRESS
Progress is saved in your browser when its local-file storage policy permits.
If saving is blocked for local HTML in your browser, you can still complete
lessons and quizzes during the open session.
